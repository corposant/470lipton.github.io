The Home

Built in 1912.
Rebuilt for now.

A hundred-and-thirteen-year-old two-storey that has been taken apart and put back together — the proportions and the bay window kept, everything else new.

The main floor reads as one long, bright room. A bay window pulls morning light across the front, the living area opens to a dining space anchored by a slatted white-oak feature wall with a linear fireplace set into it, and the kitchen sits at the back — white cabinetry below, warm oak above, quartz counters, brushed-gold fixtures, and full stainless appliances. A rear entry off the kitchen opens onto the deck.

Three bedrooms upstairs, including a primary at 11'2" × 11'7", plus a small flexible room at the back that works as a nursery, office, or dressing room. The bathroom is finished with a double vanity, arched mirrors, gold fittings, and a fully tiled tub and shower.

Downstairs the basement is finished and carpeted with a blacked-out open ceiling, a second full bathroom, a separate laundry room, and a 10' × 16'4" rec room — 378 square feet of genuinely usable space on top of the 1,247 above grade. Out back: a 17'7" × 11'6" deck under a cedar pergola with lattice privacy screening, a covered carport, and a detached garage off the rear lane.
