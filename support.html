Common questions

Nog keeps nogging me. How do I make it stop?

Mark the nog Done, or snooze it from the notification. To pause everything for a while, touch and hold the Nog app icon and choose Silence. To make nogs gentler for good, pick the Whisper or Easing profile in Control Panel → Nogs & alerts.

I'm not getting nogs.

Check Settings → Notifications → Nog, and whether a Focus is on. In Nog, Control Panel → Permissions shows what's allowed.

The app is too much on my senses.

Turn on Control Panel → Calm → Low stimulation. It turns off glows, motion and vibration. Nog also follows the iPhone's Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast and text size settings.

Where are my nogs stored?

On your iPhone, or in your own iCloud Drive if you choose that. See the privacy policy.
