Nog privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version: Nog doesn't collect anything about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you type into Nog is sent to us or to anyone else.

Where your nogs are kept

  • Each nog is a plain text (Markdown) file on your iPhone, in Files → On My iPhone → Nog.
  • If you turn on iCloud Drive storage, the files are kept in your own iCloud Drive (iCloud Drive → Nog), under your Apple Account. Apple stores them; we can't see them.
  • Settings are kept on your iPhone.

Features that use your iPhone

  • Notifications and alarms are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS.
  • Siri and Spotlight can find and change your nogs. Apple handles these requests under Apple's privacy policy; Nog doesn't send anything to us.
  • Break it down uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. It runs on the device, and nothing leaves the phone.
  • Widgets and Live Activities read a copy of your nogs that stays on your iPhone.

Children

Nog doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the app does anything different.
